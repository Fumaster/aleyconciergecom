The Story

Built in the fire.
Refined in the stillness.

Mark Tulloch ran The Sussex Arms in Covent Garden — £50K a week, a team of thirty, the pulse of London's hospitality underground. Behind the success: an undiagnosed Bipolar I condition that would eventually take everything.

His rebuilding happened in Bali — through stillness, through 10 years as COO of Bali Realty, and through a radical personal framework that turned breakdown into blueprint. That framework became Conscious Systems. His hard-won AI expertise became Bali AI Consultant.
